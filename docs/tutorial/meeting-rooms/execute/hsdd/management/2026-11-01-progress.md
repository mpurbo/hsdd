## Verdict

The method delivered: twelve phases across three lanes in about seven hours, the two lanes meeting at integration without a contract change, and a gate that is green from a fresh install today. Test-first review caught real defects at every tier (a process crash on a lost connection, a read-before-lock race mutant, a date collision between test files), and each fix carries a failing test. The real threat is the evidence chain, and it has grown since 2026-10-30: an accepted ADR, two product limits and 21 phase reviews now rest on decisions no human has seen. The second threat is that the record says more than happened. Twenty-one Learnings read `spec-updated` and changed nothing; a review rejected a display rule that then shipped; three findings exist only as titles. The proxy was asked to take every stop, and it did; what it could not do is close the loop back into governance, because nothing in the run gave a disagreement or a dropped Learning a place to land. Sync A is where that loop closes.
