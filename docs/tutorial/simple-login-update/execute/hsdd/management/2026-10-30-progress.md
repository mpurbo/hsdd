## Verdict

The method worked on this project. Two lanes built against fixtures without seeing each other and met at integration with no change. A cross-node change went from intake to three signed phases in 53 minutes. Every deferred check was closed by a named later phase, and the gate is green today. The real threat is not the code. It is that the evidence chain stops at an agent: nine reviews, three waivers and fourteen dropped Learnings were decided by an owner proxy. Until A-2 is answered, "done" here means machine-checked and agent-reviewed, not human-reviewed. The second risk is lifecycle drift. profile-api@v1 still tells any reader a guarantee the service no longer keeps, and the step that should have moved it had no owner, because closing an intake does not edit contracts.
